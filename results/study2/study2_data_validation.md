## F. SSQ scoring implementation

Kennedy, Lane, Berbaum & Lilienthal (1993) Table 4 — identical item
mapping and weights to the Study 1 pipeline (`scripts/normalize_data.py`),
applied separately to the PRE and POST item sets.

| Subscale | Items (survey numbers) | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Nausea (N) | 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 15, 16 (7 items) | × 9.54 |
| Oculomotor (O) | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 11 (7 items) | × 7.58 |
| Disorientation (D) | 5, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 (7 items) | × 13.92 |
| Total Severity (TSC) | (N_raw + O_raw + D_raw) | × 3.74 |

with, per subscale, `raw = Σ items` on the 0–3 scale, then
`N = raw_N × 9.54`, `O = raw_O × 7.58`, `D = raw_D × 13.92`, and
`TSC = (raw_N + raw_O + raw_D) × 3.74`.

Item membership (7 items per subscale; several items load on two):

- **Nausea**: general_discomfort, increased_salivation, sweating, nausea, difficulty_concentrating, stomach_awareness, burping
- **Oculomotor**: general_discomfort, fatigue, headache, eye_strain, difficulty_focusing, difficulty_concentrating, blurred_vision
- **Disorientation**: difficulty_focusing, nausea, fullness_head, blurred_vision, dizzy_eyes_open, dizzy_eyes_closed, vertigo

Change scores: `Delta_X = Post_X − Pre_X` for each of the four outcomes.

## G. Presence scale — present in the data, contrary to the handoff

The AFTER export **does** contain the same five-item, seven-point
Presence scale used in Study 1 (cols 6–10), plus a VR-experience item.
Items were mapped from the Vietnamese Likert labels
(`Hoàn toàn không đồng ý` = 1 … `Hoàn toàn đồng ý` = 7) and averaged.
Presence is reported as a **supplementary** analysis, kept separate from
the primary SSQ models.

- VR experience: Yes = 196 trials, No = 128 trials.
